Account Deletion Policy

Jobseva is developed and owned by Meritlife Technologies Pvt Ltd.

Delete your JobSeva Account

You can request deletion of your JobSeva account and associated data at any time.

How to request account deletion

  • Email us at support@jobseva.app from your registered email address.
  • Use subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: your registered email, tenant/company name (if applicable), and phone number (optional) for verification.

What we delete

We delete your account and personal data associated with it. If your account belongs to a company/tenant, deletion may require confirmation from the tenant admin to prevent accidental loss of business records.

Retention

Some data may be retained if required by law, for fraud prevention, or for legitimate business purposes (for example, invoicing records). When retained, access is restricted.

Need help?

Contact us at support@jobseva.app or via the Contact page.
